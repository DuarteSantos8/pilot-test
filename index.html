Dashboard / Spatial orientation

Drag to turn the aircraft

    How it works

    The aircraft appears in a corner of the screen, seen from the front or the back, sometimes upside down. A dashed line runs to the opposite corner. Press the two keys that fly it along the line, as the pilot sitting inside would. For example W and A together for nose down and left.

    The aircraft appears from a random side with a yellow arrow leaving its nose. Steer the way the arrow points, as the pilot sitting inside would.

    Canopy on top, dark belly underneath. Red light on the left wingtip, green on the right. Arrow keys work too.
